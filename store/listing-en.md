## Full description
Load the powder, aim and fire armored armadillos at the raccoon bandits' fortresses!

**🎯 Physics-packed sieges**
Drag back to load the cannon and release. Every armadillo has a power: split into three, explode, drill, freeze, turn into a meteor, make the fortress float… Pick your order and knock out every raccoon!

**🦝 Raccoons that fight back**
Slingers who soak your powder, handymen who repair, thieves who steal points, warlocks with magic shields and 18 huge bosses with their own attacks.

**🗺 18 worlds, 700+ levels**
From Plunder Forest to the Bandit Seas and the Kraken Fortress. Every world has Normal, **Mountain mode** (lob mortar shots over rock walls and cliffs) and **Hard mode**. With rain, storms, wind, blizzards, sunsets and nights.

**🎃 Seasonal events**
Pumpkin Night, Bandit Christmas and Pirate Summer, with exclusive bosses and skins.

**🏰 Build your fortress**
Design your base floor by floor with wood, stone, ice, iron, glass and obsidian. Brace it with struts and welds, set traps (springs, fans, turrets, shields) and defend it when the raccoons counterattack.

**🎌 Six clans**
Rising Sun, Frontier, Jade Sun, the North (vikings), the Nile and the Eyepatch (pirates): each with its own fortress style and cannon.

**🏆 And much more**
24 armadillos to collect and upgrade, 36 skins, chests, daily missions, a prize wheel, 27 achievements, master shots (double K.O., collapse!), combos and slow motion. Original drum & bass and chiptune music.

No forced ads: only optional rewarded videos. No connection required.
